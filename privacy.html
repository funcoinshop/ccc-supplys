Information we collect

We collect information you give us when you contact us or place an order: your name, business name, phone number, email address, delivery address, and the details of the supplies you request. We also keep records of our calls, texts and emails with you so we can serve your account.

How we use your information

We use your information to respond to inquiries, confirm pricing and availability, process and deliver orders, and communicate with you about your orders and account. We do not sell your personal information.

Text messaging (SMS)

CCC Supplys uses text messaging to communicate with customers about inquiries, orders, deliveries and account matters. If you give us your mobile number and agree to receive texts, we may send you text messages related to your business with us. Messages are delivered using third-party carriers and messaging providers.

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to opt out of text messages, and HELP for assistance. Opting out will stop text messages but we may still contact you by phone or email about existing orders.

Mobile information will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Information may be shared with the carriers and providers that deliver our messages, solely so the messages can be sent.

Sharing of information

We share information only as needed to run our business: with delivery and shipping providers, payment processors, and the communications providers (including telephone and text-messaging carriers) that carry our calls and messages. We may disclose information if required by law.

Data security and retention

We take reasonable steps to protect the information we hold and keep order and communication records for as long as needed for business and legal purposes.

Your choices

You may ask us to correct or delete your contact information, or opt out of texts (reply STOP) or marketing emails at any time by contacting us at (346) 337-9514 or Crosscountrycontractorssuppliesllc@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version will always be posted on this page with its updated date.
